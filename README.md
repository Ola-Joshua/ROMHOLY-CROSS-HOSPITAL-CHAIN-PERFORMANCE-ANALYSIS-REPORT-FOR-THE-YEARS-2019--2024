
Introduction

This report presents a detailed analysis of Holy Cross Hospital Chain Performance for the years 2019 to 2024. This report provides a comprehensive analysis focusing on revenue generation, patient demographics, and medical conditions. The analysis goal is to identify patterns, improve healthcare service. The analysis aims to provide actionable insights to support strategic decision-making for the upcoming year. The dataset includes Name, Age, Blood type, Medical Condition, Date of Admission, Doctor, Hospital, Insurance Provider, Billing Amount, Room Number, Admission Type, Discharge Date, Medication and Discharge Date.


  Observations

1. Combined revenue generated by all Hospitals in the year is £1,417,432,043.

2. Total of individuals admitted into the hospital for the year is 55,000 individuals.

3. 27,774 individuals are Male while 27,726 are Female.

4. The Top Hospital in terms of revenue generating is Johnson Plc with £1,084,203.

5. The Bottom Hospital with a negative closing balance for the year is Medina And Elliott Stewarts with a negative balance of £-2,633.

6. There was a surge of patients in hospitals during the year 2020t. The hospitals admitted a total of 4,832 Individuals.

7. Lipitor was the most prescribed medication for the year. 11,140 individuals were prescribed the medication.

8. Ibuprofen generated £286,359,751 in total for the hospitals.

9. Cigna Insurance group paid out £287,139,345 in medical insurance. The Insurance company paid out the most in Insurance.

10. Elective admission saw about 18,655 individuals and it was the highest form of patient admission intake

11. 6969 individuals who all have A- blood group are the highest in the hospital.

12. The highest individual medical condition is arthritis with 9308 individuals with the illness.

 Insight

1. Hospitals like Medina And Elliott Stewarts are losing money. The struggling hospitals need to cut costs or find ways to attract more patients in other to cancel out the negative balance

2. Hospitals should prepare for busy months by having extra staff and stocking up full medical supplies.

3. Disease like arthritis and heart issues are common among individuals. Hospitals should have more programs to help patients manage these conditions.

4. Medicines like Lipitor and Ibuprofen are in high demand, so hospitals should make sure they have enough stock.

5. Hospitals should keep good relationships with insurance companies like Cigna to make sure they get paid on time without issues and delays.

6. High elective admissions suggest a focus on planned treatments over emergencies.
7. Johnson Plc’s financial success could provide a model for struggling hospitals to follow.
